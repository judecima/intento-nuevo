# V21b implementation state

## CLOSED — original family-seed candidate

The original candidate combined:
- 20 random Pattern Master rounds;
- furniture family seeds generated by subset re-optimization.

It is rejected by the decisive `4058501` gate.

Three repetitions per arm on the same machine:

| Arm | Boards | Time samples |
|---|---:|---:|
| 40 random legacy | 8,8,8 | 26902, 26707, 27052 ms |
| 20 random only | 9,9,9 | 19835, 19606, 19359 ms |
| 20 random + original family seeds | 9,9,9 | 19280, 19175, 19991 ms |

Therefore:
- halving 40 -> 20 loses one board;
- original family seeds do not recover the late winning column;
- the apparent speedup is dominated by halving the random work;
- the 213-case benchmark is cancelled for this candidate.

The runtime change in `src/lib/optimizer/legacy/patrones.cjs` was reverted to the V20/main legacy implementation after this failure.

## FOLLOW-UP — direct repetitive basis

A later experimental module, `src/lib/optimizer/experimental/repetitive-family-basis.cjs`, is **not part of the failed candidate above**. It constructs direct physical two-stage guillotine recipes instead of calling `optimizar()` on family subsets.

Offline analysis indicates that it can derive the consumption vector associated with the late 4058501 geometry, but this does not yet prove full-order recovery or a performance win.

It will be evaluated as a separate V21c experiment with round-count control decoupled from family generation. No hard 40 -> 20 cap is allowed inside the family flag.
